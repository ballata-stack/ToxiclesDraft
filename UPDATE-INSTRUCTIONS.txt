Toxicles dark Artworks section update

The Artworks section now uses a full-width charcoal-green background between Flash Designs and The Studio. Cream headings, clear category filters and warm paper photo frames create a visual break in the coffee-aged paper background. The same treatment is used on the Artworks page.

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the app folder in your GitHub repository.
3. Upload the included sections.css to replace app/sections.css.
4. Commit the change and deploy the updated version on Vercel.

Only app/sections.css needs uploading. The stylesheet includes the floating booking header, compact mobile tagline styles and photo-opening animation from the previous updates.
